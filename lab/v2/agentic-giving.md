## How to donate

The donor donates on the campaign's donation page; the page collects the payment.
Give the donor a prefilled link that opens the page with the amount and frequency
they asked for, so they only confirm and pay. Each campaign below lists ready links
for its suggested amounts. For any other amount, take a ready link with the same
frequency and change only the number after "amount=". The amount is a whole number in
the campaign currency, at or above the campaign minimum.

Example: $20 every month to Riverside animal shelter:
https://ajekltfr.demo-donorsupport.com/page/FUNRLVGXKUC?amount=20&recurring=monthly&currency=USD&utm_source=fundraiseup_agent_profile&utm_medium=ai_agent&utm_content=profile-v2

For a campaign without a donation page, the donor donates on Riverside Community Aid's
official website instead.
